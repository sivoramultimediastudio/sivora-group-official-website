About the initiative

Rooted in nature and long-term care

SIVORA FARMING cultivates fruit-bearing trees, traditional Indian trees, useful green plants and seasonal vegetables. Our activities emphasize plant care, soil management, water conservation and sustainable agricultural practices.

Founders: Periyasamy & Jeyalakshmi.

Our purpose

Vision and mission

Vision

To create a healthy, green, productive and sustainable agricultural environment that preserves nature and supports future generations.

Mission

To cultivate and maintain diverse agricultural plants and trees through responsible farming, resource management, environmental care and continuous improvement.

Responsible cultivation

Our agricultural activities

Land & Soil

Land preparation, soil health, plant nutrition, mulching and suitable crop planning.

Water Management

Irrigation based on seasonal and crop needs, with water conservation in mind.

Plant Health

Routine care, pruning, weed management and monitoring for pests and diseases.

Harvest & Records

Harvesting at suitable maturity, tracking growth, maintenance and produce records.

Biodiversity

Crop diversity, traditional tree preservation and protection of beneficial organisms.

Continuous Improvement

Plantation monitoring, resource planning and appropriate agricultural technology.
