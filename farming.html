About our plantation

Rooted in nature. Growing for generations.

SIVORA FARMING – Our Plantation Garden is an agricultural and plantation initiative dedicated to cultivating fruit-bearing trees, traditional Indian trees, useful green plants and seasonal vegetables. Our approach connects nature, agriculture, environmental responsibility and long-term growth.

We aim to maintain a productive green environment through plant care, soil management, water conservation and appropriate sustainable agricultural practices.

Founders: Periyasamy & Jeyalakshmi. Business profile: October 2026.

Our responsibilities

Compliance & ethical stewardship

Responsible farming begins with responsible decisions.

Our plan calls for checking land rights, business registration, water permissions where applicable, relevant forest rules, food-safety requirements, tax and invoicing obligations, labour safety and environmental responsibilities.

Important: Sandalwood cultivation, harvesting, possession, sale and transport requirements must be verified with the competent Tamil Nadu forest authorities before any regulated activity.
