01.01 FITVERSE Little Gym Stars – Kids Foundation

Program status: Proposed • Division: GYMNASTICS ACADEMY • Primary audience: 4–7, developmental readiness

Program concept & learner outcome

FITVERSE Little Gym Stars – Kids Foundation is designed as a structured training pathway within SIVORA FITVERSE. The core learning objective is play-centred movement competence, listening and body awareness. Instruction should value consistent progress, learner confidence and accurate technique over rapid results.

Exercise and activity catalogue

1. Animal walks.

2. crawling and rolling patterns.

3. balancing on floor lines.

4. soft-mat jumps.

5. low obstacles.

6. catching and throwing games.

7. coordination circuits.

Three-stage instructional progression

Foundation: learning safe movement vocabulary and baseline skills.

Development: more repetition, stability and progression appropriate to the learner.

Progression: personalized challenges with review; no mandatory advanced pathway.

Illustrative class/session flow

Equipment and operating requirements

Small cones, soft mats, low beams, foam obstacles. Verify condition, cleanliness, correct installation and suitability before each session. Participant counts must match usable space, competence and supervision.
