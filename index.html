SIVORA FARMING

NATURE • GROWTH • SUSTAINABILITY

Responsible plantation and agricultural cultivation rooted in natural stewardship, tree diversity and practical farm care.

Our Mission

To care for plantation crops, traditional trees and seasonal vegetables while respecting soil, water and local agricultural knowledge.

Our Vision

To contribute to a healthier, more sustainable future through thoughtful land management and long-term cultivation.

What We Do — Services & Focus

  • Coconut, palmyra, mango, jamun and banana
  • Guava, lemon, moringa, amla, jackfruit and sapota
  • Vegetables: okra, tomato, pumpkin, onion and brinjal
  • Plantation maintenance and water-conscious planning

Our Core Principles

  • Soil health and biodiversity
  • Respect for natural resources
  • Responsible growth and patient stewardship

SIVORA FITVERSE

STRENGTH IN BODY. DISCIPLINE IN ACTION. PEACE IN MIND.

A proposed 40-programme initiative covering gymnastics, karate, fitness, yoga and meditation, inspired by physical education and Indian traditions.

Our Mission

To encourage safe, structured learning that builds physical capability, confidence, discipline, flexibility and mindful habits.

Our Vision

To help children, young people and adults pursue balanced wellbeing through movement, values and community-oriented training.

What We Do — Services & Focus

  • Gymnastics — balance, mobility and coordination
  • Karate — technique, self-control and respect
  • Fitness — strength, conditioning and healthy routines
  • Yoga and meditation — breathing, awareness and inner balance
  • A proposed catalogue of 40 programmes across five disciplines

Our Core Principles

  • Safety and responsible instruction
  • Discipline and inclusive participation
  • Healthy bodies and peaceful minds

Proposed initiative

SIVORA DIVYAM

உடல் • பொருள் • ஆத்மா அனைத்தும் இறைவனுக்கே

A proposed Tamil-and-English Hindu spiritual learning and cultural resource, structured around 18 wings and 108 programmes.

Our Mission

To preserve and share devotional knowledge, Tamil spiritual heritage and ethical teachings with thoughtful explanation and compassion.

Our Vision

To create an accessible spiritual encyclopedia connecting generations through authentic learning, reflection and service to society.

What We Do — Services & Focus

  • Hindu devotional traditions and philosophical learning
  • Tamil spiritual literature and cultural heritage
  • Vedic and historical knowledge, educational reference
  • Proposed 18 spiritual wings and 108 learning programmes
  • Service, compassion and community awareness

Our Core Principles

  • Devotion, compassion and humility
  • Tamil heritage and respectful teaching
  • Knowledge sharing and service to humanity
