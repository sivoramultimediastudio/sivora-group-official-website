Who We Are

A Family of Businesses with Shared Ambition.

SIVORA GROUP is the collective identity of five distinct initiatives, each with a specialist mission and purpose. Our foundations connect creative technology, clothing and personal style, agriculture, training and wellbeing, and spiritual heritage.

At SIVORA GROUP, we believe that the true purpose of business is not only growth and success, but also creating meaningful and lasting value for society.

🎬 Creative enterprise

Visual storytelling, AI-assisted production, brand communication and practical digital products.

👗 Fashion with heritage

Personal choices, occasion styling and cultural elegance with customer dignity.

🌿 Care for the land

Fruit crops, traditional trees, vegetable cultivation and responsible plantation management.

🥋 Healthy communities

Proposed structured education in movement, martial arts, exercise, yoga and mindfulness.

🪷 Knowledge and service

Proposed devotional scholarship, Tamil spiritual heritage and compassionate learning.

Strength in Body. Discipline in Action. Peace in Mind.

Five Disciplines • 40 Proposed Programs

Gymnastics Academy — 8 proposed programmes

Movement foundations, coordination, flexibility and safe skill progression.

  • Foundation movement, body awareness and balance
  • Progressive coordination and controlled mobility
  • Equipment suitability, qualified coaching and supervision
  • Age-appropriate skill assessment and confidence building

Karate & Self-Defence Academy — 8 proposed programmes

Respect, self-control and responsible martial arts education.

  • Courtesy, focus, etiquette and discipline
  • Progressive technique and self-defence awareness
  • Appropriate safety measures for contact activities
  • Karate is presented with accurate Okinawan/Japanese heritage attribution

Fitness & Physical Training — 8 proposed programmes

Strength, mobility and sustainable activity habits.

  • Foundational conditioning and movement confidence
  • Posture, mobility, strength and endurance awareness
  • Appropriate recovery, supervision and progress tracking
  • Clear limits: not a substitute for clinical care

Traditional & Modern Yoga — 8 proposed programmes

Indian traditions, mindful movement and breath awareness.

  • Accessible asana and mobility pathways
  • Breath awareness and safe progressive practice
  • Voluntary cultural learning in an inclusive setting
  • Respect for personal comfort and instructor competence

Meditation & Mindfulness — 8 proposed programmes

Attention, reflection, relaxation and balanced habits.

  • Mindful attention and beginner-friendly practice
  • Calm routines, reflection and self-awareness
  • Non-coercive participation and personal choice
  • Professional safeguarding and realistic outcomes

Planning status: 5 learning divisions × 8 proposed programmes. Scheduling, instructor qualifications, safety arrangements and enrolment are subject to confirmation. The programmes do not imply medical treatment or guaranteed certifications.
