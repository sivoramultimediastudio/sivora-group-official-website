03

Generate environments, approved product concepts, characters and camera movements; check continuity and accuracy.

Stage Output: AI clips and supporting assets.

04

Assemble clips, composite assets, animate logos/titles, apply transitions, effects and colour grading.

Add approved Tamil or English narration, licensed music, sound effects and balanced audio.

Stage Output: Review draft with audio.

06

REVIEW

Client Review & Quality Control

Check spelling, brand identity, factual claims, visual continuity and contracted revisions.

Stage Output: Approved master commercial.

07

DELIVER

Final Delivery & Campaign Support

Export contracted MP4 formats, included cutdowns and basic platform publishing guidance.

Stage Output: Campaign-ready deliverables.

Deliverable Definitions & Production Standards

Master Film: The approved primary advertisement at the contracted duration.

Aspect-Ratio Export: Separate 9:16 vertical or 16:9 landscape framing of the same approved master, not a new concept.

Cutdown: Shorter promotional edit derived from approved master material; no new concept or shoot.

AI Scene: A planned visual segment combining generated clips, graphics, client media and editing.

Voiceover: One approved synthetic Tamil OR English voice where included; additional languages quoted separately.

Resolution: Full HD 1080p standard; optional 4K only where technically feasible and separately agreed. Upscaled video is not native 4K.

Exclusions: Raw AI generations, editable timelines, prompts, source files and paid-media management unless contracted.
