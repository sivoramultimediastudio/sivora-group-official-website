Who we are

More than fashion — a complete style experience

SIVORA TRENDZ is a modern fashion and lifestyle brand founded by PERIYASAMY & JEYALAKSHMI . Our purpose is not simply to sell clothing, but to understand each customer’s occasion, style, preference, size and budget — and help them create the right complete look.

We connect contemporary fashion, traditional Indian clothing, accessories, jewellery, personalized styling and customer-focused service. We believe fashion expresses identity, confidence, comfort and culture.

Our philosophy: “We bring Style and Tradition together, earn Trust through Quality, and build lasting Relationships.”
